Use these prompts in ChatGPT (DALL-E), Midjourney, or Adobe Firefly.
After generating, replace Unsplash images in the site with your custom images.

## Tips
- Generate at 1792x1024 (landscape) for hero / wide sections
- Generate at 1024x1024 (square) for card illustrations
- Use PNG export for transparent-background images
- To keep brand consistency, regenerate with: "warm gold tones, white background, premium minimal aesthetic" added to any prompt
